### Campaign matter

From [bizowizo](https://www.bizowizo.ai/en/manage-campaigns)

Summarise the project and its value clearly in a few sentences.

Volatco Multicomputer is a compact, massively parallel computer for builders who need local, direct control of their systems. It brings 288 independent F18A computing nodes, arranged across two GA144 chips, to one experimental platform. Volatco is made for hands-on work in real-time control, robotics, sensing, edge systems, and parallel-computing research—without requiring a cloud runtime or a heavyweight operating system.

* Explain the starting point, solution, implementation and intended use of support.

Volatco starts from a practical problem: builders of responsive physical systems often have to work through general-purpose computers, complex software layers, and remote services that are poorly suited to direct, predictable experimentation. The Volatco Multicomputer offers a different starting point—a compact system containing 288 independent F18A computing nodes that can work locally and in parallel.

Campaign support will fund a focused production run of the Volatco Multicomputer, including components, assembly, testing, the updated flash configuration, preparation of setup material, and fulfillment. The result is intended as a dependable experimental platform for builders, researchers, educators, and small teams exploring real-time control, robotics, sensing, local edge systems, and parallel computing.

* **Make an uncommon computing architecture available.** Your support helps put a physical, hands-on massively parallel computer into the hands of people who want to explore the GA144 and Forth-native approach directly, rather than only read about it.

* **Back an understandable, local alternative.** Volatco is designed for work where computation stays close to the system being observed or controlled. It gives builders a way to investigate low-latency, event-driven behaviour without making a cloud service or heavyweight operating system the centre of the experiment.

* **Turn a working design into a supported baseline.** Funding does more than purchase a board: it supports the manufacturing, testing, updated flash configuration, documentation, and first-run guidance needed to make a specialised hardware platform useful from the first experiment.
