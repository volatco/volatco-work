## Required before approval

The campaign manager requires the following before submission:

1. A cover image
2. At least one additional gallery image
3. One published and processed pitch video, no longer than 60 seconds
4. At least one project document (PDF, JPG, or PNG; maximum 15 MB)
5. At least one valid reward

The corporate video is a useful optional asset. The approval checklist specifically requires the pitch video, not the corporate video.

## Recommended campaign setup

### Campaign title

**Volatco**

Keep the title short. Let the cover image and description establish the product name as **Volatco Multicomputer**.

### Funding goal

**€20,000 / 20,000 coins**

Public explanation:

> This goal funds the first regional production run of the Volatco Multicomputer: assembly, testing, the updated flash configuration, setup material, and fulfillment preparation.

Do not describe €20,000 as the full cost of all 125 possible units. It is the amount required to start a safe, viable run.

## Rewards: work with the platform's fixed bands

Bizowizo allows one reward in each of these bands:

| Band | Recommended use |
|---|---|
| €1–€100 | Optional digital thank-you only; do not promise a physical product. |
| €101–€250 | Leave unused unless a genuine, low-cost reward is available. |
| **€251–€500** | **Volatco Multicomputer at €449; limit: 125.** |
| €501–€1,000 | Leave unused at launch. A multi-board reward must not be added until its allocation can be deducted from the 125-board total. |
| €1,001–€5,000 | Leave unused at launch. |

## Asset plan

### Cover image

Use a photograph or accurate render of the exact Volatco Multicomputer offered. It must visually establish the physical board first; the existing illustrated banner can be used as a secondary campaign visual, not the sole product proof.

Suggested overlay:

> VOLATCO MULTICOMPUTER
>
> 288 independent computers for local, real-time experimentation

### Gallery images

Upload at least these three images:

1. Exact offered board, front view, with major connectors visible.
2. Exact offered board, installed and connected to the external USB–FTDI setup.
3. Simple architecture graphic: two GA144 chips → 288 F18A computing nodes → local control, sensing, and experimentation.

Avoid imagery of stacking, unreleased variants, or hardware not included in the reward.

### Required project document

Create and upload a short PDF titled:

> **Volatco Multicomputer — Technical & Fulfilment Overview**

It should contain:

- Product photograph and exact contents of the reward
- Core architecture: two GA144 chips and 288 F18A nodes
- Updated flash configuration, described in confirmed practical terms
- Host/setup requirements, including the external USB–FTDI interface
- Shipping regions, estimated delivery, support, and contact details
- A plain statement of exclusions: no integrated USB interface unless specified; no stacking capability promised

### Required 60-second pitch video

**0–06 s — problem**

> When a machine needs to react, sending every decision through a cloud service is not always the answer.

**06–16 s — product**

> Meet Volatco Multicomputer: a compact platform with 288 independent computing nodes.

**16–32 s — show the board operating**

> Two GA144 chips let builders explore local, event-driven, Forth-native computing—close to sensors, controls, and experiments.

**32–45 s — intended uses**

> It is built for real-time control, robotics, sensing, edge systems, and parallel-computing research.

**45–55 s — campaign promise**

> This campaign funds a focused production run of the Volatco Multicomputer, with updated flash and practical getting-started material.

**55–60 s — call to action**

> Support the first 125 Volatco Multicomputers and help put local parallel computing into more hands.
