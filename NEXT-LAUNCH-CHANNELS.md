# Volatco: Next Launch Channels

## Decision

Use crowdfunding to validate and finance the first **125 Volatco Multicomputer VOL00-01b** boards, then continue selling the same product through a maker-hardware marketplace and our own site. The product is a single 32 MB board offer; VOL00-01c is not part of this launch.

## Priority order

| Priority | Channel | Purpose | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Crowd Supply](https://www.crowdsupply.com/apply) | Main crowdfunding launch | Curated hardware platform with an engineering/maker audience and post-campaign distribution. |
| 2 | [Lectronz](https://lectronz.com/sell) | Ongoing direct sales / capped preorder fallback | European marketplace for specialist and open-source electronics; supports Stripe and EU VAT/IOSS tools. |
| 3 | Volatco website | Our permanent product home and email list | We own the audience, documentation and future revision sales. Link out to the live purchase channel. |
| 4 | [BackerKit Crowdfunding](https://www.backerkit.com/) | Alternative campaign platform | Use only after building a sizeable pre-launch follower list; it has campaign and pledge-management tools. |

Do not publish duplicate simultaneous crowdfunding campaigns. Keep one funding destination at a time and point every other channel to it.

## Common launch packet — prepare once

- Final product name: **Volatco Multicomputer — VOL00-01b**.
- Unambiguous specification: two GA144/288 nodes and **32 MB SPI flash**.
- State exactly what is and is not in the box, including that the external USB–FTDI interface is required and whether it is included.
- Working prototype photographs and a short demonstration video.
- Technical PDF updated for VOL00-01b; do not show or offer VOL00-01c.
- Build, test, packaging and delivery plan for 125 units.
- Costed price, shipping regions/rates, delivery estimate, VAT/import treatment, warranty/returns policy.
- Founder/team identity, business and payment details suitable for Stripe verification.
- Campaign story: what the multicomputer is, who it is for, why this run matters, risks, and the delivery timetable.
- A launch email list / waitlist and a clear way to contact us.

## 1. Crowd Supply — main route

### Before applying

- Have a real, working prototype and show it in clear photos/video.
- Have a credible manufacturing quote, bill of materials, test plan, delivery schedule and shipping plan for the 125-unit cap.
- Be ready to explain user rights, documentation and the product's technical value. Crowd Supply is particularly suited to original, useful, respectful/open hardware.
- Set a funding goal that covers the actual minimum production run, platform fee, fulfilment, payment fees, VAT/tax exposure, packaging, contingencies and returns.

### Go-live criteria

- Crowd Supply accepts the application and agrees the creator statement of work.
- The campaign page has approved explanatory copy, images/video, pledge levels, funding goal and deadline.
- The promised product, pricing, shipping regions and delivery date are feasible. Crowd Supply performs a project review; do not commit to a date or cost that cannot be delivered.

### Notes

- Crowd Supply says it typically charges 12% of campaign sales and can continue sales/distribution after the campaign.
- It is non-exclusive after original campaign fulfilment, so Lectronz and our own store remain available afterwards.

## 2. Lectronz — sales and preorder route

### Before listing

- Create the seller account and connect a verified Stripe account.
- Prepare a listing with accurate product description, photographs, price, stock/preorder quantity, shipping weights/rates and lead time.
- Decide whether to list as in-stock, or as a clearly labelled preorder with the delivery window.
- Be able to stock, pack, dispatch and support orders ourselves; Lectronz does not fulfil for the seller.

### Go-live criteria

- Seller/payment account is approved.
- Product listing passes Lectronz review.
- Tax, shipping and returns information is complete. For EU sales, use its VAT/IOSS settings as appropriate to our tax position.

### Notes

- Store creation and listing are free; the first five orders are commission-free, followed by a 5% marketplace commission excluding taxes, plus Stripe processing fees.
- Use a hard inventory limit of 125 minus any boards already committed through a completed campaign.

## 3. Volatco website — permanent home

### Go-live criteria

- Product page with a clear “Join the waitlist” or “Buy / preorder” action leading to the currently active sales channel.
- Technical documentation, software/source links and a contact email are live.
- Privacy notice and mailing-list consent are present before collecting email addresses.
- Never accept paid orders directly until payment, tax, fulfilment, returns and consumer-law obligations are set up.

## 4. BackerKit Crowdfunding — alternative route

### Before submission

- Complete creator profile, Stripe connection and identity verification.
- Build a pre-launch page and obtain enough genuine followers. BackerKit normally requires a “Somewhat Likely” funding status: followers representing at least 30% of the pledges needed to fund.
- Prepare title, image, description, at least one story section and at least one pledge level.
- Import prior crowdfunding projects if applicable. A first-time creator can ask BackerKit for an override.

### Go-live criteria

- Page passes BackerKit review (allow two to four business days).
- Stripe and identity verification are complete.
- The follower/likelihood check passes or BackerKit grants an explicit override.

## Gate before any public launch

Proceed only when all are true:

- [ ] One production-ready VOL00-01b prototype has been demonstrated and documented.
- [ ] The 32 MB specification, price, quantity cap and inclusions are identical across every public page.
- [ ] Manufacturing cost and shipping calculations support the chosen funding goal and selling price.
- [ ] The 125-unit allocation is tracked in one place, with no overselling across channels.
- [ ] A named person can answer customer messages and publish campaign updates.
- [ ] No personal or payment data is submitted to an unverified platform merely to test its editor.
